Aftercare rituals

to keep your skin glowing — follow our simple self-care routine

Each step is designed to prolong your clinical results and make your beauty routine more mindful.

Cleansing and washing

Use a lukewarm, pH-balanced amino acid cleanser morning and evening to respect your skin’s delicate lipid barrier.

Serum care routine

Press 3-4 drops of antioxidant and hyaluronic serum onto damp skin immediately after cleansing for deep hydration.

Moisturize & Protect

Seal hydration with a ceramide cream, followed by broad-spectrum mineral SPF daily to defend cellular longevity.

Gentle Exfoliation

Incorporate mild fruit enzymes or lactic acid once weekly to encourage smooth renewal without micro-abrasions.
